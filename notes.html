Leadership & Sports

Managing output vs. coaching outcomes.

These days, you can’t just give your product team a strict set of rules and measure their success by how many features they deliver. Real leadership is more like coaching right alongside them. You need to help your team stay aware of what’s happening so they can adjust quickly when things change, and keep everyone focused on the end goal instead of just following a script.

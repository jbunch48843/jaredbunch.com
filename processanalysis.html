Overview

Problem & Solution

Users need reports of the simulations to share with stakeholders, but static reports would not provide real-time insights. If they wanted to make changes, they would have to generate the report, adjust their design, and re-run the simulation to see the impact of those changes. This process wastes time and forces users to switch back and forth between the static report and the design, possibly many times.

I looked at how we might add more value to the user and streamline this workflow. That solution was to integrate real-time simulation monitoring into the design process. This allowed users to adjust their designs while the simulation was running and immediately see the impact.
